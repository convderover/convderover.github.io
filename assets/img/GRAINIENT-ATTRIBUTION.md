## Resolution and retrieval limitation

These files are the directly exposed 325 x 238 JPEG preview representations of the listed free assets. Cold Ocean's actual Download control was clicked, but produced no completed browser download or new tab (20-second event timeout). The 6K original advertised on its detail page was not recovered. Removing CDN resizing query parameters returned the same 325 x 238 image, so `grainient-cold-ocean-22-full.jpg` is NOT a full-resolution original and must not be represented as such.

For the site, use a dark overlay and blur or blend treatment as a low-frequency texture. Do not stretch it as a sharp full-screen photograph.
